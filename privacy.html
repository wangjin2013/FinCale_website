FinCale Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

FinCale is an offline financial calculator for iPhone and iPad. You do not need an account, and we do not operate a server that stores information about you.

FinCale is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. Because of this, the app does collect some data — not for us, but for our advertising partner. Sections 3 and 4 explain exactly what and why.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account, email address, or any personal details.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs of our own, and we do not build a profile of you.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell the amounts, rates, dates, cash flows or other values you enter for calculation.
  • We do not sell your data.

Your worksheet inputs, settings, memories and last answer are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

3. Information Collected by Our Advertising Partner

Ads in FinCale are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), where you have allowed tracking;
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (typically city or region level);
  • Device and connection details, such as device model, operating system version, language, and network type;
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped.

This data is used to deliver and measure advertising, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to detect and prevent fraud. It may be combined with data collected by Google across other apps and websites.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Your Choices: Consent and App Tracking Transparency

Where required, the app asks for your consent to personalised advertising using Google's User Messaging Platform. It may also ask whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. The app works either way.

  • If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you.
  • If you ask the app not to track, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using your advertising identifier. Google may still use limited data, such as your IP address and general location, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

You can change the tracking decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier from the same screen. Consent choices for personalised advertising can be changed from the app's settings where applicable.

5. What the App Stores on Your Device

FinCale stores the following locally so the app works offline and remembers your work:

  • Worksheet inputs, display settings, the ten memory slots and the last answer;
  • A cached copy of the Recommended Apps catalogue and its images, so the screen loads quickly and recently loaded content remains readable offline;
  • A local flag recording whether the ad-removal purchase is active.

This data stays on your device, is not uploaded to us, and is removed when you delete the app. Worksheet data can also be cleared inside the app under Settings.

6. Recommended Apps

The Recommended apps screen downloads a public catalogue and images from wangjin2013.github.io. This is a standard web request: it contains no calculation data or personal details you entered. As with any web request, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the content.

7. Purchases

The optional ad-removal purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your card number or billing address. The app keeps a local record of whether the purchase is active and verifies it with StoreKit.

8. Children's Privacy

FinCale is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not configured to serve personalised ads to users we know to be children.

9. Data Security

We do not operate a server that stores your information, so there is no store of user data on our side that could be breached. Data held by Google as our advertising partner is protected under Google's own security practices. Locally stored data is protected by iOS and your device passcode.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the revision date will be updated. Please review this page periodically for any changes.
