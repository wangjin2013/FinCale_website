FinCale

A financial calculator for iPhone and iPad: time value of money, cash flows, bonds, depreciation, statistics and more — all computed on your device.

Features

🧮 11 Worksheets

Time value of money, amortization, cash flow (NPV, IRR, MIRR, payback), bonds, depreciation, statistics and regression, percent change, interest conversion, dates, profit margin and break-even.

🔢 Scientific Calculator

Trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, logarithms, powers, roots, factorials, combinations and permutations, ten memories (M0–M9), constants, and last-answer reuse across worksheets.

📶 Works Offline

Calculations and worksheets need no network connection. Inputs and settings are saved on your device and survive a restart.

🔒 Private by Design

No account. The amounts, loans and cash flows you enter stay on your device and are never uploaded.

🌏 Chinese & English

The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese, with configurable decimal places, separators, angle unit and date format.

📱 iPhone & iPad

A native layout that adapts to iPhone and iPad, including landscape arrangements. Requires iOS 15.0 or later.

Important Notes

For Reference Only

Results are computed from published financial textbook formulas and are provided for reference only. They are not a basis for investment decisions, financial accounting, or tax filings. Verify your inputs and results before relying on them.

Independent

FinCale is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any calculator manufacturer. It uses standard financial mathematics and its own interface design.
