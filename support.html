Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a loan payment?

Open Worksheets → TVM. Mark Payment as the value to solve for, then fill in the periods (N), annual rate (I/Y), present value (PV), future value (FV), payments per year (P/Y) and whether payments are at the end or beginning of each period. Tap Calculate Payment.

Are the results accurate?

Worksheet results use standard financial formulas from published textbooks and are computed at full internal precision. The amortization schedule rounds each payment and interest amount to the cent, as a bank does, so it can differ from the TVM result by a few cents. Results are for reference only — verify your inputs and results before signing a contract, filing taxes or posting entries.

Does FinCale work offline?

Yes. Calculations, worksheets and the calculator work without a network connection. Advertisements and the Recommended apps screen need network access.

How do I remove the ads?

FinCale is free and supported by ads. Settings → Remove Ads offers a one-time, non-consumable purchase that removes every ad permanently on devices signed in with the same Apple ID. If ads come back after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Worksheet inputs, settings, the ten memories and the last answer are saved locally and are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them; Settings → Clear all worksheet data clears the worksheets while keeping the memories.

Why is the payment shown as a negative number?

FinCale follows the standard cash-flow convention: money coming in is positive and money going out is negative. For a loan, the amount borrowed is positive and the payments are negative. Keeping the signs consistent is what makes the math work.

What do I need to run FinCale?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 15.0 or later. The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

The Recommended apps screen is empty or out of date

That screen downloads a public catalogue and images, so it needs a network connection. Pull down to refresh. Content is cached, so previously loaded entries may still appear while offline.

Is FinCale affiliated with a calculator manufacturer?

No. FinCale is an independent app. It uses public-domain financial mathematics and its own interface design, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any calculator manufacturer.

How do I clear one worksheet or everything?

Each worksheet has a Clear this worksheet button at the bottom. Settings → Clear all worksheet data removes the inputs of every worksheet. Restore defaults resets only the display format, leaving worksheets and memories untouched.
